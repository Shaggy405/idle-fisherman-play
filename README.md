# Idle Fisherman

A fishing game that can play itself. Fish Mill Pond by hand, or turn on Auto-Fish and the fisherman works the bank on his own, chasing jumping baitfish, weed beds and big shadows; you choose the bait, the rod and the rest of the gear.

**Play it now: https://shaggy405.github.io/idle-fisherman-play/**

This is an early tester build: one pond, fished on foot, and all the art is a stand-in.

The game tells you when there is a newer build: a notice appears at the top with a "Reload now" button. To do that it reads one small file from this site every few minutes; it sends nothing. "What's new", in the bottom corner, lists what changed in each build.

Your progress is saved in the browser you play in.

## Found a problem, or have an idea?

Press **Send feedback** in the bottom corner of the game. It opens a new report here, already filled in with where your game stood (the build, your level, your gear), so all you need to add is what happened and what you expected. Then press "Create" (called "Submit new issue" on some screens). You need a free GitHub account to send it.

**My reports**, beside it, shows the reports you have sent and any reply to them.

You can also write one from scratch on the [Issues](https://github.com/Shaggy405/idle-fisherman-play/issues) page.

The build you are playing is shown in the bottom corner.
